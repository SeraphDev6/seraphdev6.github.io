Case Study

Decide — decision support for neurodivergent users

ITI Assistive Technologies

Decision fatigue is heaviest exactly when the stakes are high. We built Decide with ITI Assistive Technologies to lower that load — with special consideration for neurodivergent users — without ever taking the decision out of the person's hands.

Before Decide

A hard choice arrives as a wall: options, trade-offs, and pressure all at once. Cognitive load spikes, and the decision stalls or gets made under stress.

With Decide

The app breaks the choice into structured steps, surfaces the trade-offs one at a time, and hands the final call back to the user — clarity without a hand-off of control.

The AI structures the decision; the person makes it. That design line is the whole point — and it's the same principle we bring to every project.
